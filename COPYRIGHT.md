## 3. Notice & Takedown Policy (DMCA / Copyright Infringement)

If you are a copyright owner, artist, or an authorized representative thereof, and believe that any illustration in Esvigy infringes upon your copyright or intellectual property, **we will act immediately to remove the contested artwork.**

We operate an expedited, zero-friction takedown process:

### How to Submit a Takedown Request

You may submit a takedown request through either of the following channels:

1. **GitHub Issue (Recommended for transparency)**:
   - Open an issue at: **[github.com/nickfrostech/esvigy/issues](https://github.com/nickfrostech/esvigy/issues)**
   - Use the title: `[Copyright Takedown]: <IllustrationName>`
2. **Direct Email**:
   - Send an email to: **`nb.nicholasbenson@gmail.com`**
   - Use the subject line: `[Copyright Takedown Request] Esvigy Illustration: <IllustrationName>`

### Information to Include in Your Notice

To help us process your request quickly, please provide:

1. **Identification of the Contested Illustration**:
   - The component name or SVG file path in Esvigy (e.g. `packages/svg/src/ExampleIllustration.svg` or `ExampleIllustration` in `@esvigy/react`).
2. **Identification of the Original Work**:
   - A link to your original artwork, portfolio page, publication date, or other reasonable evidence demonstrating your ownership.
3. **Contact Information**:
   - Your name, email address, and (if applicable) company or portfolio website.
4. **Good-Faith Statement**:
   - A statement affirming: *"I believe in good faith that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law, and that I am the copyright owner or authorized to act on their behalf."*

---

## 4. Takedown Actions & Response Timeline

Upon receiving a valid takedown notice:

1. **Immediate Quarantine/Removal**: We will promptly remove the contested illustration from the source repository (`packages/svg/src/` and `packages/svg/unprocessedSVG/`).
2. **Component Removal**: We will remove the component from the `@esvigy/react` export barrel and re-publish updated package versions.
3. **Website Update**: The illustration will be immediately purged from the live Studio at [esvigy.vercel.app](https://esvigy.vercel.app).
4. **Zero Dispute Policy**: We do not engage in adversarial disputes over community-submitted artwork. If an illustration is disputed by an artist or copyright holder, our default action is immediate, permanent removal.
